🏎️ AlphaTrax TCG — Collect. Evolve. Race to Stage 6.AlphaTrax TCG is an original, 100% screen-free vehicle evolution trading card game created by Shivoham Dash in Bengaluru, India. Players collect, trade, and evolve vehicles through Stages 1 to 5 to battle across Speed, Thrust, and Aerodynamics—with the ultimate goal of completing a full evolution chain to redeem a real-world Stage 6 Thrust SSC Prize Card shipped to their home.🌟 Key FeaturesOriginal Evolution Mechanics: Stack vehicle cards from Stage 1 up to Stage 5 to compound stats and outpace opponents.Real-World Trophy Redemption: Completing a physical 5-stage evolution line unlocks the serialized Stage 6 Thrust SSC Prize Card.Interactive Web App (index.html):Custom Racing Favicon: High-contrast slanted "A" icon optimized for minimized browser tabs.Interactive Battle & Evolution Simulator: Playable track simulator right on the site to test card stats and lap showdowns.Interactive Price Menu & Order Builder: Browse Luck, Guaranty, and Booster packs plus single Stage cards, generating pre-filled WhatsApp orders.Stage 6 Verification Portal: Form with photo upload preview and ticket generator (#AT-002+) for prize claims.Founder Story: Showcasing the journey from initial sketches to debuting at Kidzepreneur 2.0 (Essensai 067, Whitefield).📁 Repository Structurealphatrax-tcg/

On your turn, stack Stage 2 through Stage 5 on top to increase stats.Stat Showdown: Place active cards face down. The turn player calls a stat: Speed (KM/H), Thrust (HP), or Aerodynamics (Aero). Reveal simultaneously—highest stat wins the lap.Victory: Win 5 laps to win the match.
