How to Play AlphaTrax TCG

AlphaTrax is a fast-paced, 100% screen-free vehicle evolution card game. Battle opponents across 3 key stats: Top Speed, Horsepower/Thrust, and Aerodynamics!

1

Deck & Setup

Each player builds a custom 10 to 20 card deck containing base vehicles (Stage 1) and higher evolution cards (Stages 2–5). Draw 5 cards to start your pit crew.

5 Hand Cards
2

The Evolution Chain

Play a Stage 1 vehicle onto your track. On future turns, stack Stage 2, Stage 3, Stage 4, and Stage 5 directly on top to multiply your speed & power stats!

Stage 1 ➔ Stage 5
3

Stat Showdown

Both players place their active car face down. The turn player calls a stat (Speed, Thrust, or Aero). Reveal together—highest stat wins the lap!

Face-Down Reveal
4

Track Points & Stage 6

First player to win 5 laps takes victory! Collect all 5 physical Stages of an evolution line outside the game to claim the real-world Stage 6 Thrust SSC!

Claim Real Prize Card

About the Founder

Built from Scratch by a Young Bengaluru Creator

Most trading card games are built in massive corporate boardrooms. AlphaTrax TCG was built from the ground up by Shivoham Dash, a young creator in Bengaluru with a big imagination, a passion for high-speed vehicles and evolution mechanics, and the drive to turn a simple idea into a real-world game.

What started as hand-drawn vehicle stats and evolution chains—where players collect Stages 1 through 5 to unlock the ultimate Stage 6 Thrust SSC Prize Card—quickly grew into a hands-on entrepreneurship journey.

At Kidzepreneur 2.0 at Essensai 067 in Whitefield, Bengaluru, Shivoham brought AlphaTrax out onto a real tabletop in front of players and families, proving that young creativity paired with hard work can turn into a product kids and collectors love.
